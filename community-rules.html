// COMMUNITY RULES

Cheer, don't sneer.

Pacr's feed is for runners cheering each other on. Five rules keep it that way. Breaking them gets the post removed and can get the account closed — we don't run a warning system for abuse.

01

No abuse, ever

No harassment, threats, hate speech, or slurs — about anyone, in a post, a comment, a photo or a squad name. There is no warning system for this. Posts go, and accounts go with them.

02

Nothing sexual

No sexual or explicit content, and nothing involving minors. Photos included.

03

No spam or scams

Don't sell things, farm follows, or post links you wouldn't send a friend.

04

Post your own runs

Share runs you actually did, and photos you actually took. Don't post someone else's picture or a route that isn't yours.

05

Respect the squad

A squad is somebody's room. Owners can remove posts and members, and set the feed to owners-only. Public clubs are open to anyone, so hold them to the same standard.

If you see something

In the app, long-press any post or comment. On the web, open the ··· menu on the post. Either way you can report it or block whoever wrote it.

Reporting hides it from you straight away and sends it to the squad owner to review. Blocking is immediate and mutual: neither of you sees the other's posts or comments, and they can't comment on or like yours. Undo a block any time from Profile → Blocked accounts in the app.

We review every report. Anything abusive comes down, and the account behind it can be removed.

Contact us

Something urgent, or a report you think we've missed? Email us and we'll come back to you.
