## 1. Context in 30 seconds

Pacr is an AI running coach app (iOS + Android, currently Play Store
closed alpha) launching from Bangalore. It coaches rather than tracks:
daily AI workout prescriptions, voice-first in-run coaching, AQI/weather
awareness. The website's job is **not** app installs — it's club signups
(city running groups) as the top of funnel. Full strategy: `WEBSITE_PLAN.md` §0.

## 2. The job

Rebuild the single-page site at pacr.life to match `WEBSITE_PLAN.md`:

- **Structure**: the six sections in plan §4 (hero → what Pacr is → the
  runs → app preview → social proof → early access CTA → footer).
- **Brand**: the **acid system** (`brand-acid/tokens.css` + the design
  system reference page). This replaces the current site's look entirely.
- **CTAs**: club signup form (primary) + early access form (secondary),

- Keep the page static, fast, single HTML+CSS(+minimal JS). It deploys

## 4. Design decisions — defaults chosen, override knowingly

1. **Body font = Inter.** The app uses JetBrains Mono for body, but mono
   paragraphs hurt marketing readability. Inter is the spec'd body family
   (product spec §5). Mono stays for labels/captions/data.
2. **Hero headline is sentence case** ("Your city runs with Pacr.") in
   Barlow Condensed 900 italic with the -6° skew — the acid display style
   without the uppercase. ALL CAPS is reserved for mono labels (≤2 words)
   and CTA buttons (≤3 words — so the hero CTA is `JOIN YOUR CITY`, not
   the plan's 5-word draft; plan §6 rule wins over plan §4 draft copy).
3. **Photos get the ink-scrim treatment** borrowed from the app's share
   card: full-bleed photo, solid `--pitch` scrim behind any text, mono
   caption, optional acid hairline. No rounded photo corners.
4. **One cream section allowed.** The page is dark (`--pitch`), but one
   section — social proof is the best candidate — may flip to `--paper`
   with ink text to break the wall of dark. Use `wordmark-inverse.svg`
   there. No more than one.
5. **Acid discipline.** One acid element per viewport. Stripes/tape
   sparingly — at marketing scale they skew aggressive (product spec §16
   warns about this even in-app).
6. **Accessibility floor**: don't ship text below the `--dim` tier;
   tokens.css explains why the app's lightest tier is excluded.

## 6. Voice (non-negotiable)

Calm coach, not drill sergeant. Sentence case body. No exclamation
points. No "soldier / earn / grind / crush" language. Present tense
("Pacr runs happen on weekend mornings" — never "launching" or "waitlist",
except "Joining soon" for inactive cities). India-specific: name cities,
reference AQI, morning-run culture. Full rules: `WEBSITE_PLAN.md` §6 +
product spec §2 in the app repo.
