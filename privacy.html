01 / Introduction

The shortest version we could write.

Pacr is an apparel brand and an invite-only running app. We collect the minimum information needed to ship you garments, run our app, and keep our community honest. We don't sell your data. We don't run ad networks against you. This policy explains what we do collect, why, and what control you have over it.

"Pacr", "we", "us" and "our" refer to Pacr Life Ltd. "You" refers to anyone who visits pacr.life, buys from us, or uses the Pacr app.

02 / What we collect

Information you give us, and a little we observe.

We collect three kinds of information:

  • Information you give us — name, email, shipping and billing addresses, phone number, sizes, payment details (processed by our payment partner, not stored by us), and any messages you send to support.
  • Information from the Pacr app — training sessions, paces, distances, heart rate (if you connect a wearable), goals, and any notes you write. This data is encrypted at rest and tied to your account only.
  • Information collected automatically — IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, and approximate location (city-level). We use this to keep the site fast and detect abuse.
03 / How we use it

To ship, to run, to improve. Nothing more.

  • Fulfill your orders and process returns.
  • Operate the Pacr app — your training feed, pace insights, and coach connections.
  • Send order updates, restock alerts (if you opt in), and the occasional editorial drop note.
  • Detect fraud, prevent abuse, and meet our legal obligations.
  • Understand which products and features people actually use, in aggregate.

We do not use your training data to target ads. We do not sell your information to data brokers.

04 / Sharing

A small set of partners, each for a specific job.

We share information only with vendors who help us run the business:

  • Payments — Stripe processes cards; we never see your full card number.
  • Shipping — DHL, USPS, and regional carriers receive the address needed to deliver.
  • Email — our transactional and marketing email provider sends your receipts and (if opted in) journal notes.
  • Analytics — privacy-respecting analytics that do not use third-party cookies or personal identifiers.
  • Legal — when required by law, court order, or to protect our rights.

We don't share your data for anyone else's marketing. Ever.

05 / Cookies

Just enough to keep you logged in.

We use a small number of first-party cookies for session state, cart persistence, and aggregate analytics. You can clear them at any time from your browser. Where required by law, we'll ask for your consent before setting any non-essential cookies.

06 / Your rights

You own your data. You can take it back.

Wherever you live, you can:

  • Request a copy of the information we hold about you.
  • Correct anything that's wrong.
  • Ask us to delete your account and associated data.
  • Withdraw consent for marketing emails at any time (one click in any email).
  • Object to or restrict certain types of processing.

Residents of the EU, UK, and California have additional rights under GDPR, UK GDPR, and the CCPA respectively, including the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Email privacy@pacr.life to exercise any of these. We'll respond within 30 days.

07 / Retention

We keep what we need, for as long as we need it.

Order records are kept for seven years to meet tax and warranty obligations. Training data lives as long as your Pacr account is active; you can delete your account from in-app settings, after which we erase identifiable data within 30 days (some anonymous, aggregated records may remain).

08 / Security

Encrypted in transit. Encrypted at rest.

All traffic to pacr.life uses TLS. Production databases are encrypted at rest. Access to customer data is logged and limited to staff who need it to do their job. No system is unbreakable — but we hold ourselves to a standard our members would expect.

09 / Children

The Pacr app is for adults.

Pacr is intended for users aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you believe a minor has provided us with information, contact us and we'll remove it.

10 / Changes

If something material changes, we'll tell you.

We may update this policy as the business grows. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent version. If we make material changes, we'll notify members by email at least 14 days before they take effect.

11 / Contact

Talk to a human.

Privacy questions go to privacy@pacr.life. General support is hello@pacr.life. Postal mail: Pacr Life Ltd., Privacy Office.
