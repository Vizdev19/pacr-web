01 / Who we are

Who we are.

Pacr ("we", "us", "our") is an AI-powered running coach app. You can reach us at:

Email: support@pacr.life
Website: https://pacr.life

02 / Quick summary

The short version.

We try to collect the minimum data necessary to run the app:

  • Anonymous by default — you can use Pacr without an email or any identifying information. The app creates a random anonymous account on first launch.
  • Optional email — only if you want to recover your account on a new device or create a private squad.
  • Your run data is yours — we sync some of it to our servers so it works across devices, but only you and people you explicitly invite to your squad see it.
  • We never sell your data.
  • No ads.

04 / Location data

Location data.

Pacr requests device location to:

  • Track your GPS route during a run
  • Show weather and air quality (AQI) at your location
  • Suggest nearby running spots

When we access location

  • Foreground only: while the app is open on the Home or Run screen
  • Background: only during an active run, so your route is recorded if you lock your screen. Tracking stops automatically when the run ends.

What we send

  • GPS routes: stay on your device only. Never uploaded to our servers.
  • Coarse location (rounded to ~1 km grid): sent to a weather and air-quality data provider to fetch a forecast for your area. No personal identifier is attached.
  • Distance to running spots: computed entirely on-device.

You can revoke location permission at any time via your device settings. Pacr remains usable without location, but run tracking, weather, and AQI features will be disabled.

05 / Physical activity data

Physical activity (motion sensor) data.

Pacr uses your device's accelerometer to measure running cadence (steps per minute) during a run. This data is processed entirely on-device and is not transmitted externally.

06 / How we use your data

How we use your data.

We use the data described above to:

  1. Run the core app: track runs, generate workout prescriptions, calculate streaks, show progress
  2. Power the squad feature: display the weekly leaderboard to people you've invited to your squad
  3. Improve the app: aggregate, anonymised analytics help us see which features are used
  4. Communicate with you: we don't send marketing emails. Email is used only for authentication (one-time codes for sign-in / sign-up)
  5. Comply with legal obligations: respond to lawful requests if required

We do not use your data to build advertising profiles.

08 / Squad data and visibility

Squad data and visibility.

When you create or join a squad:

  • Your display name becomes visible to other members of that squad
  • Your weekly run count, total distance, and current streak become visible to other members of that squad (this is the leaderboard)
  • Your individual GPS routes, addresses, and personal records are not shared with squad members

When you leave a squad, your historical leaderboard entries for that squad's past weeks remain visible to other members, but you no longer contribute new data.

09 / Email authentication

Email authentication (optional).

If you choose to link an email to your account:

  • We send a one-time code to that email address
  • We use the email only to identify your account on sign-in attempts — never for marketing
  • You can unlink the email at any time from Profile → Account

Linking an email is required to create a new squad (so your squad doesn't become orphaned if you reinstall the app). Email is not required to join a squad via invite code, to use the AI coach, or to track runs.

Deleting your data

You can delete your data in two ways:

  1. In-app — open Profile → Delete all my data. This wipes your on-device data and signs you out. Server-side records for your account are queued for deletion within 30 days.
  2. By email — write to support@pacr.life from the email linked to your account. We will delete all server-side records associated with you within 30 days and confirm by reply.

Uninstalling the app deletes your on-device data but does not delete your server-side account. Use the in-app option or email us to remove that.

Where your data is stored.

Pacr operates globally. Data may be stored or processed in multiple regions, including India, the European Union, and the United States. When data crosses borders, we rely on appropriate legal safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses) and on the privacy commitments of our service providers.

12 / Your rights

Your rights.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your data (see Section 10)
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Port your data to another service
  • Withdraw consent for optional features (e.g., linked email)

For most data, you can exercise these rights directly in the app. For anything else, email support@pacr.life and we will respond within 30 days.

If you're in India, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 applies. You may lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Board of India if we don't respond to your request.

If you're in the European Union or United Kingdom, you may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

13 / Children's privacy

Children's privacy.

Pacr is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect data from such children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us at support@pacr.life and we will delete it promptly.

14 / Security

Security.

We protect your data using industry-standard practices:

  • All network traffic is encrypted in transit
  • Your account data is encrypted at rest
  • Authentication credentials are stored using your device's secure storage
  • Strict access controls so squad members can only see data for circles they belong to

No system is perfectly secure. If we become aware of a breach affecting your data, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

Continued use of the app after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
