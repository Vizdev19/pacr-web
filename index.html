How it works

Pick a race. Run the plan.

Three steps to a coach in your pocket. After that, Pacr does the thinking — you just lace up.

01

Set your goal & race

Tell Pacr your target — 5K PB, first half marathon, finish-line photo at TMM. Five minutes, no jargon.

02

Get your week of running

Easy runs, intervals, long runs and rest — laid out across the week and reshaped each morning around how you slept and recovered.

Strava and Garmin sync automatically. Pacr reads every run — pace, HR, perceived effort — and recalibrates next week's load.
