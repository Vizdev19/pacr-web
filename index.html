01 / Daily plan

Three inputs. One session.

The plan is rebuilt at 4am, every morning. No static 12-week block that ignores the fact you slept badly and it's 33°C outside.

INPUT 01
Your last run

Split drift, cadence, and how hard the end felt. If Sunday cooked you, Monday is 40 minutes easy — not the interval set you planned in January.

LOAD: 78 / RECOVERY: PARTIAL
INPUT 02
Your real pace

PACR models threshold from your own sessions, not a calculator. Targets move week to week, in seconds, not in tiers.

THRESHOLD: 4:28 /km ▲ 6s
INPUT 03
Today's climate

Heat, humidity, wind on the exposed stretch. The session gets the same stimulus at an honest pace instead of a broken one.

HEAT OFFSET: +14s / KM
02 / Voice coach

A coach in your ear, not a chart to read later.

PACR calls the next block before you get there, pulls you back when you drift 8 seconds hot, and shuts up when you're holding it. Answer out loud — "cut it short today" — and the session reshapes mid-run.

Block calls two beats before the change, so you settle instead of lurch.
Pace nudges only when you drift past your own tolerance band.
Say "cut it short" or "one more" — the plan rewrites mid-run.
Silent stretches on purpose. Coaching, not commentary.
